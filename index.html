About IITH Yenagoa

The International Institute of Tourism and Hospitality (IITH), Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, was established in 2012 by the Bayelsa State Government. It is NBTE-accredited and offers National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND) programmes in Tourism, Hospitality, and allied disciplines.

Rector: Professor Apuega R. Arikawei
