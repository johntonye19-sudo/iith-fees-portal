Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

We collect information necessary to process fee payments and identify students (e.g. name, matriculation number, contact details, payment references).

Data is used only for fee collection, receipting, audit, and institutional administration. We do not sell personal data.

In production, data is stored on secured systems with access limited to authorised bursary and IT staff. Transmission should occur over HTTPS.

Payment card data, when used, is handled by the payment service provider (PCI-DSS); the portal should not store full card numbers.

For requests regarding your data, contact support@iithyenagoa.edu.ng.
