Bursary management roadmap

  • Staff auth — role-based access (Bursar, Cashier, Auditor)
  • Fee schedule CRUD — publish ND/HND/CVES rates per session
  • Invoice engine — bind student + fee + session; immutable references
  • Paystack webhooks — auto-mark paid, store gateway reference
  • Reconciliation — match bank settlements vs portal ledger
  • Reports — daily collection, by programme, outstanding balances
  • Receipt reissue — verified reprint with watermark
  • Audit trail — who changed fees, who voided a payment
