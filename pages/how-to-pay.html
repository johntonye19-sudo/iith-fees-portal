How to pay your fees

  1. 1

    Sign in or activate

    Returning students log in with matriculation number and password. Newly admitted students activate with name, email, and JAMB/application number.

  2. 2

    Open Pay Fees

    From your dashboard, choose the fee type (school fees, acceptance, form, levies, etc.).

  3. 3

    Generate e-teller / invoice

    Review the amount and system charge, then generate your payment reference.

  4. 4

    Pay online or at the bank

    In production: pay by card via the integrated gateway, or transfer using the reference shown on the invoice. Do not pay to personal accounts.

  5. 5

    Print your receipt

    After successful payment, print or save the official receipt for clearance and registration.
